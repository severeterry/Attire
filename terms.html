Terms of Service

Last updated August 2026. A plain-language summary of the terms for using Attire while the platform is in its early, founder-run stage — not a substitute for legal advice, and it will be revisited as the platform grows.

Membership

Attire membership is by application. Submitting an application doesn't guarantee approval — applications are reviewed by the Attire team on a rolling basis. Approved members agree to represent themselves and their organization honestly in their profile, applications, and any listing information.

Acceptable use

The Exchange, The Co-Op, Intros, and Feed are for real sourcing needs, collaboration, and community activity among members — not spam, misrepresentation, or bad-faith listings. Attire reserves the right to remove content or suspend an account that violates this, at the team's discretion.

Deals made through Attire

Attire is a network that helps members find and connect with each other — it is not a party to any sourcing agreement, Co-Op arrangement, or transaction members make with one another, and doesn't currently process payments on members' behalf. Members are responsible for their own agreements, terms, and payment arrangements with each other.

Dues & trial terms

Current pricing, trial length, and founding-member terms are described on the Dues & Tiers page. Founding-cohort terms (where offered) are locked in from your own join date, as described at the time you're offered them.

Account termination

You can request account deletion at any time by emailing hello@attirenyc.org. Attire may suspend or terminate an account for violating these terms or for bad-faith use of the platform.

Changes to these terms

As Attire adds features (like payments) or grows past its founding-member stage, these terms will be updated to reflect that, and members will be notified of material changes.

See also our Privacy Policy, or reach out directly with questions.
