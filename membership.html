Member Benefits

What membership unlocks.

The Exchange

Post a sourcing need or open request, and respond to other members' — visible only inside Attire.

Direct introductions

Request a warm introduction to another member instead of a cold outreach email.

Event access

Member events extend Attire's original NYC sustainable fashion event community.

The Co-Op

Member-negotiated rates on shipping, insurance, fabric testing, and other shared costs.

Who Should Apply

Built for every link in the chain

Attire membership is open to organizations working anywhere in NYC's sustainable fashion supply chain — not just brands.

  • Fabric mills & textile innovators
  • Manufacturers & cut-and-sew houses
  • Recycling & disposal companies
  • Slow fashion designers & stores
  • Secondhand marketplaces
  • Consultants, NGOs & institutions
