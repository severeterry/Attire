The Membership Network

A curated, application-based community where brands and suppliers join by being vetted. Members get access to The Exchange, direct introductions, and a seat in the network that's grown out of Attire's NYC event series.

  • Access to The Exchange Post a sourcing need or respond to another member's request
  • Direct introduction requests to other members Skip the cold email and request a warm introduction instead.
  • The Co-Op — group buying power on shipping, insurance & more Access rates negotiated across the whole membership on shared costs.
  • Access to member events and the peer community Get priority invitations to Attire's ongoing calendar of NYC meetups and mixers.
