Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how Attire handles your data while the platform is in its early, founder-run stage — not a substitute for legal advice, and it will be revisited as the platform grows.

What we collect

When you apply for membership, we collect your name, organization, contact details, borough, category, and anything you share in your application. Once you're a member, your profile, posts, messages, Exchange listings, Co-Op activity, and intro requests are stored so the platform can function — that's the whole point of the product.

How we use it

Your data is used to run your membership: showing your profile and Directory listing, connecting you with other members through The Exchange, The Co-Op, and Intros, and sending you notifications about activity relevant to you. We don't sell your data, and we don't run third-party advertising or tracking on the platform.

Who can see what

Your Directory listing is public. Feed posts, Exchange listings, and Co-Op pools are visible to other members per your membership tier. Direct contact info (email/phone) is only shared through Intros once a request is mutually accepted, and you can turn off contact-info sharing entirely in your profile's Privacy settings.

Where it's stored

Attire runs on Supabase (Postgres) for data storage and authentication, and Hostinger for hosting. We don't currently process payments — no payment or billing information is collected at this time.

Your choices

You can update or remove most of your profile information directly in your account settings. To request a full account deletion or ask what data we hold about you, email hello@attirenyc.org and we'll handle it directly — there's a small team behind this right now, so expect a real person, not an automated process.

Changes to this policy

As Attire adds features (like payments) or grows past its founding-member stage, this policy will be updated to reflect that, and members will be notified of material changes.

Questions? Reach out directly.
