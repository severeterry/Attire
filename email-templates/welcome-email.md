Hi {{contact_name}},

Welcome to Attire — you're officially part of the network.

We built Attire because the sustainable fashion industry has a coordination problem: innovators struggle to scale, small brands struggle to get funded, and almost everyone struggles to find the right connection at the right time. You're now part of the group we're building to fix that, one real relationship at a time.

**What your membership includes**

- **The Exchange** — post a sourcing need or an open deal, and respond to other members' listings.
- **The Co-Op** — pool buying power with other members on materials orders, equipment access, logistics, workspace, and shared service costs like certification audits.
- **Direct Introductions** — request a warm introduction to another member instead of a cold email.
- **The Feed** — a running view of what's happening across the network.
- **Your directory listing** — a public profile other members and visitors can find and reach out to.

Your first 6 months are free. No charge until then, and you can cancel anytime after.

**What to expect from us**

We're early, and we're building this deliberately rather than trying to grow fast and thin. That means:

- You'll hear from us personally, not just automated emails — we're actively involved in making the first introductions and pools work.
- We'll ask for your feedback, and we'll act on it. This platform is being shaped by its first members.
- We're going to reach out directly in your first month to make sure you're getting real value, not just a login.

**Where we are right now**

Attire's directory currently maps {{directory_count}} organizations across NYC's sustainable fashion supply chain — materials and making, circularity and disposal, strategy and expertise, advocacy and community, and retail and creators. The membership platform you just joined — The Exchange, The Co-Op, Intros, and the Feed — is live and built for real use, not a demo.

**Where we're headed**

Our near-term focus is making sure early members like you get genuine value: real introductions, real Co-Op pools that actually close, real sourcing connections through The Exchange. As that proves out, we'll grow the network further — more members, deeper category coverage, and eventually beyond NYC.

Thank you for being an early part of this — it means more than a signup to us.

If you have any questions at all, just reply to this email. It reaches me directly.
